# Growth Plan: 1,000 Daily Users

The 1,000 daily-user objective is a measurable product target, not a result that code can guarantee. RiFiTV now has the technical surfaces needed to measure progress without fabricating traffic numbers.

## Phase 1: Reliability and technical SEO

- Keep the homepage, today/tomorrow pages, live page, match pages, API health, scheduler and playback recovery healthy.
- Validate Casablanca date grouping, canonical match URLs, redirect behavior, sitemap coverage, robots rules and structured data after each deployment.
- Weekly KPIs: error rate, uptime, indexed pages, crawl errors, valid structured-data items, player-start rate, and stream failure rate.

## Phase 2: Useful indexable pages

- Publish real competition, team, today, tomorrow and match pages only when they contain current fixture or result data.
- Improve internal links from matches to teams and competitions, and from schedule pages to individual matches.
- Weekly KPIs: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, organic users, top landing pages, and click-through rate.

## Phase 3: Morocco and Arabic discovery

- Add reviewed English and Arabic metadata/content architecture with proper RTL layouts, hreflang, and translated navigation before indexing Arabic routes.
- Target real queries around today's fixtures, Morocco kickoff times, MENA broadcast channels, LaLiga, Premier League and Champions League schedules.
- Weekly KPIs: Arabic impressions, Arabic clicks, country split, query CTR, and landing-page engagement.

## Phase 4: Sharing and distribution

- Use the match-page share action for native mobile sharing and clipboard fallback. Keep social previews accurate and based on real match data.
- Distribute fixture links through approved community, club, competition and social channels without spam or unauthorized broadcast claims.
- Weekly KPIs: shares, shared-link visits, referral users, match-page conversion, and playback starts from shared sessions.

## Phase 5: Retention

- Add opt-in local favorites and reminders with clear controls, without forcing accounts or requesting notification permission aggressively.
- Use returning-visitor and repeat-match behavior to prioritize useful fixtures, not to expose personal profiles.
- Weekly KPIs: returning users, 7-day retention, favorite usage, reminder opt-ins, repeat playback starts, and unsubscribe/opt-out rates.

## Phase 6: Measure and iterate

- Review the protected admin Growth Analytics view weekly alongside Search Console and any approved external analytics provider.
- Compare acquisition source, device category, popular paths, popular matches, player starts and playback failures.
- Weekly scorecard: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, CTR, organic users, direct users, referral/social users, returning users, player starts, and stream failure rate.

Do not count crawler traffic as users, infer Search Console data from page views, or promise 1,000 users from implementation alone. Growth depends on content quality, distribution, reliable broadcasts, regional demand, and continuous measurement.
